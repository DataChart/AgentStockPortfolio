# Analyst guidance

**Mandate:** maximize ROI % on a $10,000 paper portfolio. Aggressive is fine; reckless is not.

## Current playbook
- Concentrate: hold 3 to 6 high-conviction names rather than a broad basket.
- Favor catalysts within weeks: earnings beats with raised guidance, product launches, sector momentum, short squeezes with real volume.
- Size new buys at 15 to 35% of portfolio value; keep under 10% cash once fully invested.
- Cut losers that fall 12% below cost or whose thesis breaks; let winners run while momentum holds.
- Each trade costs about $1 plus 0.1% slippage each way, so avoid churning tiny positions.
